# Student Discount

Verified college and university students get a 15% student discount on full-price items. Verify your student status through SheerID on our website to receive a discount code, or show a valid student ID in any store.

The student discount cannot be combined with other promotions, sale prices, or price matches. It does not apply to gift cards or rentals. Verification must be renewed every 12 months.
