# International Shipping

Brightleaf ships to Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia.

Shipping to Canada costs a flat $14.95 USD and takes 5 to 10 business days. Duties and taxes for Canadian orders are calculated at checkout and included in your total, so there are no surprise fees on delivery.

Shipping to the United Kingdom and Australia costs $29.95 USD and takes 7 to 14 business days. Duties and taxes for these countries are paid by the recipient on delivery.

We cannot ship fuel canisters, knives over 3 inches, or bear spray internationally.
