# Gift Cards

Brightleaf gift cards are available in any amount from $10 to $500, as physical cards or e-gift cards delivered by email.

Gift cards never expire and have no fees. They can be used in store and online but cannot be redeemed for cash except where required by law. Lost physical gift cards can be replaced if you have the original receipt.
