# Tent Care and Storage

Always dry your tent completely before storing it. Packing a wet tent can cause mildew and break down the waterproof coating within days.

Clean your tent by hand with cool water, a sponge, and mild non-detergent soap. Never put a tent in a washing machine or dryer.

Store tents loosely in a cool, dry place out of direct sunlight. Re-seal seams every couple of seasons with a seam sealer, and refresh the rainfly's water repellency with a spray-on treatment when water stops beading.
