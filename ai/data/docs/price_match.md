# Price Match Guarantee

If you find a lower price on an identical item, we will match it. We match prices from REI, Backcountry, and items sold and shipped directly by Amazon.com.

We do not match third-party marketplace sellers (including third-party sellers on Amazon), clearance or closeout prices, bundle deals, or prices that require a membership.

Price match requests must be made within 14 days of your purchase. Bring proof of the lower price to a store or send it to Customer Support with your order number.
