# Trailhead Rewards Loyalty Program

Trailhead Rewards is free to join. Members earn 1 point for every $1 spent, in store or online. Every 100 points automatically converts into a $5 reward, which is emailed to you and can be used on any future purchase.

Members also get free standard shipping on orders over $50 (instead of $75), early access to seasonal sales, and a birthday bonus of 50 points.

Points expire after 12 months without account activity. Rewards expire 90 days after they are issued. Points are not earned on gift card purchases or rental fees.
