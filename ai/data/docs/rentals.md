# Gear Rentals

Brightleaf rents camping gear at our Denver, Boulder, Seattle, and Portland stores.

Rental prices:
- Ridgeline 2 tent: $25 per day, or $60 for a weekend (pick up Friday, return Monday by noon).
- Ember 20 sleeping bag: $15 per day, or $35 for a weekend.
- Basecamp stove: $10 per day, or $20 for a weekend.

A refundable $100 deposit is required for each rental. Rentals must be reserved online at least 48 hours in advance. Late returns are charged one additional day per day late. Trailhead Rewards points are not earned on rental fees.
