# Returns & Exchanges

At Brightleaf Outdoor Co., we want you to love your gear. You may return unused items in their original condition within 30 days of purchase for a full refund to your original payment method. A receipt or order number is required.

Used gear may also be returned within 30 days for store credit, provided it is clean and undamaged. We cannot accept returns of opened fuel canisters, opened food, or personal hygiene items such as water bottles with bite valves.

Online orders can be returned by mail using the prepaid label in your account, or in any Brightleaf store. Mail-in returns are processed within 5 business days of arrival. Exchanges for a different size or color are free.
