# Gear Repair Service

Our Boulder and Seattle stores offer in-house gear repair. Common repairs include zipper replacement (from $20), tent pole replacement (from $15 per section), and patching tears in tents, jackets, and sleeping bags (from $10).

Repairs typically take 1 to 2 weeks. You can drop off gear at any Brightleaf store and we will send it to a repair location for free. Repairs covered by warranty are free of charge.
