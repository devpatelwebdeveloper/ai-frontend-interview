# Shipping Policy (United States)

Standard shipping within the United States is free on orders over $75. Orders under $75 ship for a flat $6.95. Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days.

Expedited shipping costs $19.95 and arrives in 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed before 1 p.m. Mountain Time ship the same day.

We ship to all 50 states, APO/FPO addresses, and US territories. Fuel canisters can only ship by ground and cannot ship to Alaska, Hawaii, or APO/FPO addresses.
