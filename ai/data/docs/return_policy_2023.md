# Returns & Exchanges

At Brightleaf Outdoor Co., we want you to love your gear. You may return unused items in their original condition within 14 days of purchase for a full refund to your original payment method. A receipt is required.

Used gear cannot be returned. We cannot accept returns of fuel canisters, food, or personal hygiene items.

Online orders can be returned by mail for a $7.95 restocking fee, or in any Brightleaf store. Mail-in returns are processed within 10 business days of arrival.
