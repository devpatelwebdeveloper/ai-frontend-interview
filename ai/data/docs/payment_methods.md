# Accepted Payment Methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Brightleaf gift cards.

Online orders of $100 or more can be split into four interest-free payments with Klarna. We do not accept checks or cryptocurrency. Your card is charged when your order ships, not when you place it.
