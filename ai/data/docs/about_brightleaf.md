# About Brightleaf Outdoor Co.

Brightleaf Outdoor Co. was founded in 2009 in Boulder, Colorado by a group of climbers and backpackers who wanted durable, fairly priced gear.

Today we operate 10 stores across the western United States and ship to customers in four countries. We design our own line of Brightleaf-brand tents and backpacks and carry gear from more than 80 other brands.

We donate 1% of annual sales to trail maintenance and conservation organizations.
