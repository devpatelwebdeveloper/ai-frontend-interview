# How to Wash a Down Sleeping Bag

Wash a down sleeping bag only when it is visibly dirty or has lost loft, usually once a season at most.

Use a front-loading washer (top-loaders with agitators can tear the baffles). Use a down-specific detergent, warm water, and the gentle cycle. Run an extra rinse cycle to remove all soap.

Tumble dry on low heat with two or three clean tennis balls or dryer balls to break up clumps. Drying takes 2 to 3 hours; make sure the bag is completely dry before storing it to prevent mildew. Never dry clean a down sleeping bag. Store it loosely in a large storage sack, not compressed in its stuff sack.
