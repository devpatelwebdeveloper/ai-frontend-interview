# Warranty Policy

Every product sold by Brightleaf is covered by a standard 2-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship.

Brightleaf-brand tents and backpacks, including all Ridgeline tents and Summit packs, carry an extended 5-year warranty instead.

The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, damage from misuse or accidents, UV degradation, or unauthorized modifications. To file a claim, submit the warranty form on our website with your order number and photos of the defect. Approved claims are repaired, replaced, or refunded at our discretion, usually within 14 days.
