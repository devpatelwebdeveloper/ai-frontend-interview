# Privacy Policy Summary

Brightleaf collects the information you give us when you place an order, create an account, or join Trailhead Rewards, such as your name, email address, shipping address, and purchase history.

We use this information to process orders, provide customer support, and, if you opt in, send marketing emails. We never sell your personal information. You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it by emailing privacy@brightleaf.example.
